# Sam lends a drill

Sam's neighbour knocks: can he borrow the drill for the weekend? Sam opens the app on the [list of loans](screen:home) and taps Add Loan.

On the [form](screen:add-loan), Sam types "Drill" and the neighbour's name, skips the return date and confirms. The [confirmation](screen:confirm) shows the loan.

Monday, the drill is not back. Sam wants a reminder.

> Gap: no screen sends or shows a reminder for a late loan.

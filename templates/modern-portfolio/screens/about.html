About

I'm Noa. I design interfaces and write the code that ships them.

I trained as a graphic designer, learned front-end to stop losing details in handoff, and have worked on both sides of it since 2019.

I like the parts most people skip: empty states, errors, keyboard paths, the old tablet on a slow network. That is where a product earns trust.

Away from the screen: bouldering, film photography, and a sourdough starter older than most of my projects.

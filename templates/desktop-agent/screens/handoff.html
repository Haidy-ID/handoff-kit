Q&A

How long is a slot held during payment?
Ten minutes, then it goes back to the other members.
Can a member cancel?
Up to 2 hours before the session, refunded as credit.
What does the QR pass hold?
A signed booking id. The front desk scans it; no personal data is in the code.

Open questions

Can a member book for a friend?The brief does not say. A yes adds a guest field to Slot and a second pass.

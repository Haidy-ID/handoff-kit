# Switch

A setting that applies at once, with no Save button. Native checkbox with `role="switch"` in its label.

```html
<label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span class="switch__track"><span class="switch__thumb"></span></span>Dark mode</label>
```

- The thumb springs to its side; pressing stretches it toward where it will go.
- A choice saved later with a form: `checkbox`.
